Know what to look for.

Practical checklists to use before you shortlist a property.

01 / HOME VIEWINGSWhat to check on a home viewing ＋
  • Look for signs of damp. Check ceilings, window frames, and the walls around bathrooms for stains or peeling paint. Ask about past leaks or repairs.
  • Try the everyday essentials. Open windows and doors, run the taps to check water pressure and drainage, and pause to listen for outside noise.
  • Check how your furniture would fit. Measure key rooms and doorways. Ask which fixtures, appliances, and parking spaces are included.
02 / NEIGHBOURHOODSHow to compare two neighbourhoods ＋
  • Try your usual commute. Time the door-to-door trip when you would normally travel. Include walking, transfers, and parking.
  • Visit at different times. Walk the routes you would use on a weekday and again after dark. Notice traffic, noise, street lighting, and footpaths.
  • Compare your weekly essentials. Check access to groceries, healthcare, schools, or public transport. Note which errands you could do on foot.
03 / COMMERCIAL LEASINGWhat to ask before leasing a workspace ＋
  • What will you pay beyond rent? Ask for a written breakdown of the deposit, service charges, utilities, parking, and any scheduled rent increases.
  • Can the space support your business? Ask about permitted use, access hours, internet, signage, and which alterations you can make.
  • What happens if your plans change? Ask about renewal, early exit, and what you would need to remove or restore before handing back the space.

How do I find a property?＋

Choose Buy, Rent, Commercial, or Land. Add a location, property type, and budget, then select Search properties. More filters lets you refine area, bedrooms, furnishing, and who posted it.

Can I save properties for later?＋

Yes. Select the heart on a listing, then open Your shortlist. Favourites are saved in this browser when local storage is available. They are not synced to an account.

Are these properties available to visit?＋

The properties shown are for illustration. Viewings will open when live listings are available.
